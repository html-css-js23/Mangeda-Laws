Overview & Purpose

A **Building Permit** is required for any construction, alteration, or demolition of structures over a certain size. This ensures that all projects comply with zoning laws, accessibility standards, and public safety regulations.

1. Eligibility and Criteria

All applicants must be eligible for the following criterias:

<
  • Applicant must be the registered property owner or a legally appointed agent.
  • All structures must maintain a minimum 10 BLOCK setback from the property line.
  • All applicants must make over 10 gold ingots per 10 days income.

2. Required Documents (List all necessary paperwork)

Ensure applicants know exactly what documents they must submit to avoid delays in processing.

  1. Completed Application Form (BP-101, signed and dated).
  2. Three (3) certified copies of detailed construction blueprints (1/4" scale minimum).
  3. Proof of Property Ownership (Current Deed or Title).
  4. Contractor's License and Insurance Information.
  5. Site Survey Plan stamped by a licensed surveyor.

3. Application Process Steps & Fees

Outline the flow of the application from initial submission to final inspection and permit closure.

  1. **Pre-Application Meeting:** Recommended for complex projects.
  2. **Submission:** Submit all documents to the City Planning Office.
  3. **Review Period:** Expect a review time of **4-6 weeks**.
  4. **Fee Payment:** A non-refundable application fee of $250 is due upon submission. Construction fees vary based on project value.
  5. **Final Approval & Permit Issuance.**

**Important:** Building without a valid permit will result in immediate stop-work orders and significant fines.
