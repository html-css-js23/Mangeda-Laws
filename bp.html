Overview & Eligibility

The Building Permit is required for all new construction and significant external modifications. Issuance is contingent upon compliance with property dimensions and successful Zoning Law checks.

To obtain this permit, you must first acquire a Residency Card from the Mangeda Government - Citizenship & Immigration Services.

1. Property & Structure Dimensions

All measurements are given in blocks.

  • Maximum Property Line: 30 x 30 blocks* (Width x Length).
  • Maximum Property Height: 50 blocks*.
  • Core House Footprint: Must be built on 25 x 25 blocks*.
  • Setback Requirement: This leaves a five-block gap on each side. You may build a garden in this gap, but no property structure is allowed*.
  • Roof Limits: The roof structure can only extend up to 28 x 28 blocks*.

2. Zoning Laws & Inspection

  • Zoning Approval: One of the Mangeda Government officials will personally check the area you wish to build in and will issue a Yes or No decision.
  • External Modifications: To make any modifications to your house on the outside, you require a government official to inspect the work after completion.
  • Renting Property: To charge rent, you are legally required to obtain a Selling Permit (SP).

3. Tax Zones & Rates

The location of your property determines your tax rate*:

  • High Tax Zone: On top of a mountain or on a separate island.
  • Medium Tax Zone: Next to a village, in a very flat area, or near existing buildings.
  • Low Tax Zone: On very steep hills, near forests, or inside forests.

To apply for the Building Permit, please click the Apply for Permit Now button located at the top of this page.
